#$ DESCRIPTION: Profile 2 keeps the flight data (craft and pilot name, battery voltage, core temperature, flymode, item timer, link quality, stick overlays, throttle and warnings) and drops the artificial horizon, crosshairs-style flight aids, adjustment range, rate profile name, RSSI and VTX channel, leaving room for the diagnostics.
