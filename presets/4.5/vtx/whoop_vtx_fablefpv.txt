#$ DESCRIPTION: VTX TX power control on the AUX7 switch, taken from "Whoozat" (BetaFPV Air65 65mm whoop) CLI dump of 2026-11-02 (Betaflight 4.5.0).
#$ DESCRIPTION: Each vtx row sets the TX power for a range of AUX7. The firmware evaluates the rows on every RX frame and writes the matching row power into vtx_power, so an active row always overrides a power set by hand in the Configurator.
#$ DESCRIPTION: The firmware only applies a row power when it is greater than zero, so power level 0 (the "25 " level) cannot be selected by a row - it means "leave the power as it is". The lower detent of the 2-position and 5-position options is therefore left unmapped on purpose, and the option sets vtx_power = 0 so the hand-set value starts at 25 mW.
#$ DESCRIPTION: Result: with AUX7 in the lower position the VTX stays at 25 mW until you change it; with AUX7 anywhere else it takes that position value. After visiting a higher position the lower one keeps that value, because "leave as set" means leave whatever was last set - re-pick 25 mW in the Video Transmitter tab to go back.
#$ DESCRIPTION: Band and channel are left at 0, which the firmware reads as "do not change the current band or channel", so this preset touches neither the band table, the power ladder, nor the selected band/channel - the VTX manufacturer settings are left exactly as configured.
#$ DESCRIPTION: Choose how many power levels the switch steps through. The 3-position option reproduces the captured configuration, in which every detent forces a level. The captured dump also had two further rows that were unreachable, because row 2 covered 1500-2100 and shadowed them.
#$ DESCRIPTION: Power is a level index into the factory ladder (level 0 = 25 mW, 1 = 100, 2 = 200, 3 = 400, 4 = PIT). Levels 1 to 4 are used for forced positions because level 0 is the "do not change" value.
